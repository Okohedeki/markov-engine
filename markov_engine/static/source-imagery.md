# Source imagery

`orbit.webp` is an optimized version of NASA image `iss074e0395792`, showing a
Progress spacecraft departing the International Space Station above Earth's
limb. It is illustrative space imagery, not an image of the water system or the
2023 demonstration. No identifiable person appears.

Source: https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/iss074e0395792/iss074e0395792~large.jpg

Credit: NASA. Retrieved from the NASA website on 2026-09-06 and used to illustrate
the source-backed educational example. The page's source notes identify it as
illustrative and do not imply NASA or other source publishers endorse Markov.

Media guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The image was resized and encoded as WebP; visual cropping is done responsively
in CSS. Competitor imagery and brand assets are not included.
