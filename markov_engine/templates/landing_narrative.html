01 · Understand

First, separate what the source said from what it skipped.

A fast summary would repeat the thesis. Markov exposes the actors, assumptions, and unanswered question inside it.

Result You know what matters before you decide what to believe.

Claim from the source

“Japan’s aging population could force it to sell U.S. Treasuries.”

Starting thesis · not yet established
What remains after the headline

A policy signal and a plausible incentive—not evidence of one coordinated Treasury dump.

02 · Verify and connect

Then independent sources fill specific roles.

Markov does not draw a web of vaguely related cards. Every connection says what it contributes and where the argument can break.

Result The mechanism becomes inspectable instead of implied.

Starting thesis Japan may sell U.S. Treasuries.
  1. Reuters · Policy catalystJapan’s pension pivotSupports

    A domestic-allocation proposal exists, but its size and implementation remain unsettled.

  2. NBER · Working paperWhat about Japan?Provides mechanism

    Rates, duration, institutional mandates, and USD/JPY hedging sit between demographics and a sale.

  3. U.S. Treasury · TICMajor foreign holdersTests the event

    Observed holdings can challenge a story built only from incentives and policy language.

  4. Bank of Japan · FSRInstitutional portfolio conditionsAdds context

    Domestic financial conditions change which portfolios might rebalance and why.

Policy direction→Higher JGB appeal→Weaker hedged returns→Gradual rebalancing

03 · Follow the idea

Keep the competing paths. Choose the one worth developing.

Markov ranks directions without deleting the alternatives. You decide whether the next piece should optimize for support, originality, or consequence.

Result One source can become several different pieces—without collapsing them into one generic script.

Follow the slow reallocation, not the dump.

Higher domestic yields, policy pressure, and currency-hedging costs can change specific portfolio decisions before any coordinated national sale appears.

What would change this?

A formal allocation change followed by aligned sector-level outflows.

04 · Finish

The selected path becomes work—not a detached answer.

Change the format. The claim roles, citations, competing explanation, and unresolved question stay beside the document.

Result A draft you can edit, publish, or hand to an agent with its reasoning intact.

Japan did not decide to dump America’s debt.
