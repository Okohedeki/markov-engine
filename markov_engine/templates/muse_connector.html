The connector provides read-only access to one connected archive. Your notes, original source text, and Markov’s interpretations remain separate. Results include links back to the original sources.

It can search saves, fetch a source, list threads, open a thread, and find items worth revisiting. It cannot edit or delete your saves.

A dedicated key.

Ask your Markov administrator to enable the connector for your account. Keep connector keys private; do not paste them into a chat or commit them to Git.

  1. 01

    Enable the endpoint.

    Configure a dedicated token mapped to your archive’s existing owner ID through MARKOV_MUSE_API_KEYS. Restart Markov on a trusted HTTPS deployment.

  2. 02

    Test the connection.

    Use POST /mcp/muse with bearer authentication and an MCP client supporting Streamable HTTP. The endpoint stays disabled until configured.

  3. 03

    Complete Muse review.

    Use the repository’s setup and submission checklist to validate the required authentication, authorized origins, and end-to-end connection with Muse.
