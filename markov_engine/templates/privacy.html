This notice explains how the current Markov application handles information. Markov is operated by EyeQ enterprises in Phoenix. A self-hosted copy may use a different operator and configuration.

Information used by the service

When you use a workspace, Markov stores the links or text you submit, retrieved source material, research results, saved ideas, scripts, series, production status and edits. Account identifiers, access permissions, job history, credit transactions and usage events support access control and operation of the service.

Support messages include the information you choose to send. The server or hosting provider may also log technical request information, such as IP address, requested page, time and error details.

Why information is processed

The application uses this information to retrieve sources, find connections, produce requested outputs, save your work, manage access and credits, diagnose failures and respond to support requests. Do not submit secrets, sensitive personal information or material you do not have permission to process.

Accounts, cookies and browser storage

Where Clerk is configured, Clerk manages account credentials, verification, recovery, social sign-in and authentication cookies. Customer account pages load Clerk’s browser components. Markov checks the signed session and associates work with a user identifier; it does not store your Clerk password. Some authentication cookies are accessible to Clerk’s browser component, not HTTP-only. Clerk processes customer account data for Markov under its service agreement; direct requests about your Markov account to us. Clerk also publishes its own privacy notice.

Legacy self-hosted key sessions use an HTTP-only cookie with a 30-day browser lifetime. The older sample studio stores selected examples and edits in your browser’s local storage; you can remove that data through browser settings. The public source-to-story example does not submit a research job.

Markov does not include separate advertising or visitor-analytics scripts. This statement does not describe technical telemetry, logs or cookies used by identity providers, linked websites or a separately configured hosting service.

Source retrieval and service providers

Opening external sources connects to their publishers, whose own policies apply. Research may send relevant source text, prompts or search queries to the model, embedding and search services configured for a deployment. Local/offline configurations do not use the same providers as a cloud configuration.

Where Stripe credit-pack checkout is enabled, Stripe processes payment details; Markov stores credit and payment-event records rather than card numbers. Contact us before submitting confidential information if you need the provider list for your workspace. This notice does not promise that every deployment keeps processing on-device.

Retention and your choices

Workspace records persist in the application database so you can return to them. This build does not automatically delete records when a plan’s listed retention period ends; do not interpret a capacity label as a guaranteed deletion schedule. Browser exports remain wherever you save them.

You can export available work and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of account data. We may need to verify account ownership and identify records that must be retained for security, accounting or applicable requirements. There is no self-service account-deletion screen in this build.

Before a hosted launch

The active hosting, processor list, retention procedures and any applicable regional rights must be confirmed for the hosted service. This pre-release notice is not a claim of compliance with every privacy law or a guarantee of absolute security.
