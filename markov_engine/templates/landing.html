Useful boundaries

Built for factual work with consequences.

Specificity is part of the trust contract. Markov is excellent at a narrower job—not every kind of writing.

Best for

  • Factual creators and editorial teams
  • Analysts, consultants, and operators
  • Research-led policy, marketing, and communications
  • Developers building evidence-aware agents

Not designed for

  • Fiction, ad concepts, or generic content volume
  • A chatbot answer without a source trail
  • Academic ghostwriting
  • Sources Markov cannot lawfully inspect
