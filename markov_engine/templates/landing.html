    Made for original work

    Do not start the next piece from an empty page—or someone else’s conclusion.

    Markov helps people who publish, explain, and learn build a point of view from evidence instead of producing another summary of the same source.

    1. For creatorsFind the angle worth making.

      Turn saved TikToks, YouTube videos, podcasts, posts, and articles into original scripts for short-form video, long-form video, newsletters, and audio.

    2. For publishers and growth teamsCreate work that deserves to become the answer.

      Build source-backed explainers, AEO pages, reports, and editorial briefs with the evidence and uncertainty still attached.

    3. For researchers and curious peopleUnderstand more than the source said.

      Expose missing mechanisms, compare competing explanations, save what matters, and return when a new question opens another path.

    One workspace for the whole trail

    The source does not disappear after the output.

    Markov parses what you save, stores it in your workspace, builds and checks the research chain, and keeps every brief, report, and script connected to the claims and evidence that produced it.

    1. 01Capture and parse

      Bring in videos, articles, PDFs, podcasts, posts, or a question.

    2. 02Store and connect

      Keep sources, claims, evidence, and research chains organized together.

    3. 03Review and decide

      Inspect passages, challenge weak links, and choose which branches deserve more work.

    4. 04Edit and create

      Refine briefs, reports, scripts, and answers without losing their research trail.
