Separate the claim

“Japanese investors will dump Treasuries” is several claims wearing one coat.

Domestic yields, allocation mandates, hedging costs, and marginal demand can move on different timelines.

Domestic yields become more competitive. Mandates can alter new allocation. Demand can weaken before holdings are sold.

A deliberate fit

For people whose work has to hold up.

Markov fits

Research-led creators, analysts, strategists, and consultants.

People turning recurring research into briefs, reports, or scripts—and agent builders who need inspectable research state.

Markov does not fit

High-volume filler, unconstrained fiction, or passive read-later archives.

The source trail is part of the product. Markov does not replace the human decision to publish.
