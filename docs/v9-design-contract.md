# Markov V9 design contract

## Working brief

- **Mode:** clean-slate visual redesign of the public site and authenticated workspace.
- **Primary user:** a research-led creator, analyst, strategist, or consultant who has found a useful source but has not yet found the useful work inside it.
- **Primary outcome:** make it effortless to begin with one source and understand that Markov can carry the same trail through missing context, connections, and a finished output.
- **Functional progress:** scattered reading and manual source transfers become one continuous path from encounter to usable work.
- **Emotional progress:** the user moves from “this is interesting, but I do not know what to do with it” to a sense of orientation and momentum.
- **Social progress:** the user can produce work that feels considered, defensible, and distinctly theirs.
- **Dream:** every useful encounter can keep unfolding instead of disappearing into an archive.

## Reference principles

These are principles, not layouts to copy.

- **Outset:** confidence comes from scale, typographic restraint, long calm intervals, and real material rather than a dense component inventory.
- **Recall:** the product can occupy an immersive branded stage; depth, movement, and a legible product visual create desire faster than explanatory diagrams.
- **The Public Domain Review:** images, titles, and metadata can form a rich editorial rhythm without placing every item in a decorated card.

## Art direction

1. **Design thesis** — Markov should feel like entering a calm field where a stray source gathers depth and becomes useful work: spacious, tactile, curious, and quietly kinetic.
2. **Memory hook** — a continuous source ribbon changes form as it travels: captured media becomes an exposed missing link, then becomes a finished page without losing its provenance.
3. **Composition** — use full-canvas scenes, overlapping planes, curved transitions, large type, and long negative-space intervals. Alternate immersive stages with quiet editorial passages. Alignment may shift, but the ribbon provides continuity. Mobile must recompose the ribbon vertically rather than stack desktop cards.
4. **Typography** — use a confident grotesk for display and interface language, with tightly tuned tracking and fewer sizes. Reserve the serif for the finished document itself, so the output feels materially different from the surrounding product. Remove routine all-caps kickers and badge-like labels.
5. **Color logic** — warm fog is the dominant field; midnight ink carries reading and deep-work scenes; cobalt is the action color; ember marks the human decision or useful interruption. A single atmospheric blue field may transition from dark source ambiguity to pale clarity. Status colors remain semantic and quiet.
6. **Material language** — cropped source covers, transcript fragments, marginal notes, one floating workspace plane, soft depth, and a fine route line. Prefer image edges, type, and spatial overlap to borders. Surfaces float only when they are interactive or genuinely layered.
7. **Motion** — the source ribbon enters once, selected source material shifts with short crossfades, and the route/output stage preserves spatial continuity. No looping decoration. Reduced motion removes translations and smooth scrolling.

## Product story

1. **Recognition:** “I found something useful. I do not want it to become another saved tab.”
2. **Core action:** paste a video, article, paper, podcast, post, or question.
3. **Transformation:** follow one Japan policy source through the missing buyer mechanism and into a finished research angle.
4. **Difference:** Markov does not stop at summary; it exposes what the source assumes, follows the most useful connection, and keeps evidence attached.
5. **Outcomes:** catch up, explore, or finish a brief, report, or script.
6. **Next action:** open the working product. A lower-commitment secondary action opens the truthful sample.

## Interaction thesis

- The universal input should feel embedded in the opening scene, not mounted inside a dashboard card.
- Source and route choices update one shared stage, preserving continuity instead of replacing whole panels.
- The authenticated workspace should begin with work in motion: one generous capture control, one next decision, and plain research trails. Supporting metadata recedes until requested.

## Responsive system

- **Desktop:** one wide stage with deliberate overlap and a maximum reading measure, not a uniformly constrained page column.
- **Tablet:** reduce overlap, keep the visual stage intact, and let the source ribbon become a shallow horizontal rail.
- **Mobile:** use a quiet top bar, full-width copy, swipeable source choices, and a vertical transformation. No persistent bottom navigation or overlapping fixed chrome.
- **Accessibility:** one `h1`, semantic landmarks, labelled controls, visible focus, 44px touch targets, keyboard-operated tabs/dialogs, non-color states, and a reduced-motion treatment.

## Forbidden defaults

- Equal bordered cards or repeated rounded rectangles as the main section language.
- Boxed hero input followed by three equal outcome tiles.
- Full-width dark slabs used merely to create section contrast.
- Citron feature blocks, floating icon bubbles, glass panels, generic AI sparkle motifs, and decorative node graphs.
- Tiny all-caps kickers on every section.
- Dashboard mosaics, boxed empty states, or borders whose removal would not change meaning.
- Fake proof, invented metrics, fictional evidence, or borrowed customer logos.
- Copying Outset’s centered serif hero or Recall’s exact blue gradient/product grid.

## Acceptance test

- The first screen names the input, the transformation, the output, and the working CTA.
- Removing the Markov logo still leaves a recognizable source-to-missing-link-to-output interaction.
- The page can be described without naming a color: “the source ribbon becomes the work.”
- Desktop and mobile feel composed rather than assembled.
- The workspace uses layout and type for hierarchy before containers.
- Evidence, uncertainty, export entitlements, and existing product behavior remain truthful.
