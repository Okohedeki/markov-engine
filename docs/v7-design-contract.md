# Markov V7 design contract

Status: active direction for the public landing page and workspace dashboard.

The current product direction is a hosted SaaS workspace and API. Any older
repository guidance describing the public offer as free/open-source or local-only
is superseded by the user's canonical product brief.

## Product progress

- Functional: a saved source becomes an inspectable Chain, several research
  directions, and a brief, report, script, or newsletter.
- Emotional: the user stops losing promising material in tabs and stops wondering
  whether an original angle actually holds up.
- Social: the user can publish a point of view that is informed, traceable, and
  recognizably theirs.
- Dream: everything worth saving can keep developing until it becomes useful work.

## Public landing page

1. **Problem** - a curious creator needs a way to develop something they found
   because saving or summarizing it does not expose the missing context or the
   strongest original direction.
2. **Primary outcome** - understand source -> Chain -> defensible direction ->
   finished output, then open the workspace.
3. **Design thesis** - an expressive research studio where a visible thread carries
   one source through context, connection, and finished work.
4. **Memory hook** - the same live research thread remains attached while the user
   switches the starting signal and watches the opportunity change.
5. **Composition** - one decisive first viewport, followed by a continuous proof
   sequence rather than a feature grid; large public typography and asymmetry are
   allowed here.
6. **Typography** - compact sans display with a restrained italic serif turn for
   the human contribution; calm sans body; mono only for source and evidence labels.
7. **Color logic** - deep ink field, warm paper reading surfaces, sharp coral action,
   mineral green evidence, and cool indigo connections. Color always indicates
   source, relationship, evidence, or action.
8. **Material language** - captured-source tickets, typed connections, annotated
   evidence, research paths, and output excerpts. No stock imagery or decorative AI
   graphics.
9. **Interaction thesis** - selecting a source updates the found opportunity;
   selecting a research stage or direction updates the same Chain; reduced motion
   preserves every state without tilt or scroll-driven changes.
10. **Primary CTA** - `Open Markov`; secondary CTA - `See a Chain unfold`.

Best fit: research-led creators, editorial teams, analysts, and strategists who
publish factual work. Poor fit: generic high-volume copy, fiction, or work where
source traceability does not matter.

## Workspace dashboard

1. **Problem** - a returning user needs to capture or resume meaningful research
   quickly because the current dashboard spends too much space re-selling the idea.
2. **Primary outcome** - see what needs attention, resume the strongest Chain, or
   start a new one within three seconds.
3. **Design thesis** - a calm research operations desk: compact, legible, and
   action-led, with the density of a working tool rather than a marketing surface.
4. **Memory hook** - one `Needs your attention` ledger aligns the direction, its
   research basis, uncertainty, status, and next action in a single readable row.
5. **Composition** - persistent navigation, compact utility header, universal
   capture command, main attention ledger, and subordinate activity/recents rail.
   No hero and no promotional manifesto.
6. **Typography** - system sans throughout; 12-14px working text, 24-30px page
   heading, tabular numerals for totals, and mono only for compact status metadata.
7. **Color logic** - cool gray workspace, white reading planes, deep navy text,
   cobalt primary action, green completion, amber judgment, red failure. The public
   coral/ink drama does not carry into the app.
8. **Material language** - rows, dividers, quiet panels, stage bars, filters, and
   attached evidence summaries. Radius and shadow appear only for interactive
   floating elements.
9. **Interaction thesis** - source type changes the capture prompt and returns focus;
   status is always visible; every row has one explicit next action; mobile becomes
   a capture-and-resume surface rather than a compressed desktop dashboard.
10. **Required states** - populated attention ledger, empty direction state, active
    research stage, queue clear, disabled upload, visible focus, and reduced motion.

## Forbidden defaults

- Reusing the landing hero composition, serif scale, warm-paper field, or oversized
  scores inside the dashboard.
- Fake customer proof, fabricated outcomes, decorative network graphs, stock people,
  generic feature-card grids, gradients, glass, glows, or an ornamental terminal.
- Leading the public page with API or engine language.
- Leading the dashboard with a marketing promise instead of current state and work.
