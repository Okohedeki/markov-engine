A few useful details

A home for curiosity.
With clear boundaries.

What can I save?

Public links from any platform. Articles and PDFs can be extracted automatically. When a site blocks access or a transcript isn’t available, the link and note stay safe. Add source text or a timestamped transcript to make the important parts searchable.

Do I need to organize everything?

No. Related concepts form threads automatically. You can rename, merge, split, pin, or hide them. Projects are there when you want a little more focus.

Can I take my archive with me?

Yes. Export your archive as JSON or copy a save’s relevant context with source references. Markov’s local application is open source.

Is this a general research assistant?

Markov begins with material you chose to keep. It helps you retrieve and use your archive. It does not replace checking the original source.
