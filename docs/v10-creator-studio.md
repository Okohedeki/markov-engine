# Markov creator studio

Status: current UI direction, 2026-09-05. Supersedes V1–V9 audience and visual contracts.

The primary customer posts frequently and needs a dependable supply of ideas.
Markov starts with conversations they are following and expands the topic into
different hooks, questions, explanations, and connections worth posting about.

## Direction

1. Problem: frequent posters need many distinct ideas because finding the next
   topic and repeatedly inventing a new angle interrupts their publishing rhythm.
2. Outcome: move from one conversation to a shortlist of useful posting angles.
3. Visual thesis: an open, bright creator studio with expressive type, warm orange
   actions, and the rhythm of a contact sheet; energetic, clear, and approachable.
4. Content: conversation → angle collection → selected idea → outline → copy/export.
5. Interaction: changing a topic reveals different angles; selecting an angle
   opens its hook and outline; saving builds a visible, reversible shortlist.
6. Constraints: keep FastAPI/Jinja and existing jobs; no invented live trends,
   popularity counts, customer proof, scheduler, or direct social publishing.

## Art direction

- Memory hook: one discussion fans into several different posts, visible together.
- Composition: generous open public canvas; compact studio with topic rail,
  scannable angle collection and a contextual writing sheet. Recompose on mobile.
- Type: neutral sans for all controls and body; large, rounded, regular-weight
  headings. No compressed headings, document-led serif system, or tiny UI copy.
- Color: near-white canvas, charcoal type, orange action, muted peach/mint/lilac
  to distinguish content lenses. No glow, glass, or atmospheric gradient.
- Material: real text, hooks, content format labels, selections, and editable
  outlines. A bounded sheet is appropriate for an actual post preview.
- Forbidden: research-compliance hero, oversized dashboard headline, faux live
  feed, boilerplate feature tiles, ratings, fake counts, decorative network graph.

## Product story

- Functional: expand a discussion into a batch of different posting possibilities.
- Emotional: replace the empty-calendar feeling with useful options.
- Social: show up often with an angle of your own.
- Dream: a repeatable publishing rhythm with room for original thought.
- Brand: Creator, with Explorer as a secondary influence; curious, direct, enabling.
- Best fit: creators, social teams, personal brands, and agencies posting often.
- Poor fit: users expecting automatic social publishing from this build.
- Primary CTA: open the studio. Public static builds lead to the working example.

## Scope and truthfulness

The interactive example uses explicitly labelled, prewritten sample discussions
and angles. Its shortlist and edited outlines persist in this browser with copy
and Markdown download. It does not simulate AI generation or claim a live feed.
The authenticated studio submits real sources/topics to the existing research
job and keeps real results and drafts accessible. Live discussion discovery,
batch AI generation, and scheduled publishing remain engine integrations.

Older skill references excluding volume creators do not apply to this direction;
the user's explicit audience correction takes precedence.
