# Guided source workspace

## Current brief — 7 September 2026

This replaces the app composition in v11-soft-studio, not the public site.
The user approved three destinations: Add a link, Saved articles, Scripts.
Scripts contains saved ideas and writing progress; series is a continuation,
not a second top-level product. Audience and voice are not part of intake.

## Direction contract

- Problem: frequent creators need worthwhile, sourced angles without sorting
  through research terminology or a wall of interchangeable suggestions.
- Outcome: understand one source, choose a specific angle, inspect its evidence,
  and deliberately start paid writing.
- Visual thesis: an open, crisp reading surface beside a compact ink navigation
  rail; expressive headline hierarchy with restrained warm action color.
- Content: distinct original-source context, short rundown, numbered script
  ideas, attached passages, then a single development action.
- Interaction: jump from rundown to ideas; reveal evidence in place; keep a
  selected angle and its cost explicit before generation. No forced wizard.
- Constraints: existing FastAPI/Jinja and source data; no new framework, model
  calls on page view, invented images, fabricated hooks, or free full scripts.

## Art direction

Memory hook: the source remains identifiable while its possible stories unfold
on the main canvas. Distinct objects, one continuous journey.
Composition: narrow navigation, generous but bounded reading width, strong title
contrast, open lists separated by space and fine dividers instead of card grids.
Type: existing self-hosted DM Sans; readable 15–17px body, 28–40px titles,
13px supporting labels. No decorative serif or monospace costume.
Color: light canvas, charcoal navigation, burnt orange reserved for action and
current-step emphasis. Status always has text, never color alone.
Material: actual source titles, hostnames, excerpts, and document structure.
Use source artwork only when it exists and is safe to show; do not invent it.
Forbidden: metric tiles, decorative gradients, repeated pills, generic AI copy,
fake trend feeds, autoplay, and multiple equally prominent generation buttons.

## References inspected

- https://www.cosmos.so/ — clear content identity and generous composition;
  not its floating imagery or collection feature set.
- https://thecreativeindependent.com/ — specific editorial titles carry interest;
  not its typewriter styling or multicolored grid.
- https://culturedcode.com/things/ — navigation remains subordinate to the
  selected work and next action; not a new project-management feature set.

## Verification boundary

Prove entry, saved-source navigation, a real retained research result, evidence
disclosure, script selection, paid boundaries, and desktop/mobile rendering.
Preserve ownership checks, existing drafts, research, exports, and series data.
No new research is needed just to preview this UI.
